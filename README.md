# Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

![Dashboard Preview](Screenshots/dashboard-preview.png)

## Project Overview

I developed this interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** using **Microsoft Power BI** to provide a comprehensive analysis of sales performance, profitability, regional trends, and operational metrics for a retail business.

The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into clear, visually appealing, and actionable insights, enabling stakeholders to monitor business performance effectively and make data-driven decisions.

## Key Insights

- **Total Sales**: $4.38 Million
- **Total Profit**: $4.34 Million
- **Total Orders**: 2,000
- **Return Rate**: 0.25%
- **Total Quantity Sold**: 16,000 units
- **Total Shipping Cost**: $41.26K

## Business Highlights

- Strong profitability with an impressive overall margin
- Peak sales performance achieved in 2024, followed by a moderate decline in 2025
- Exceptionally low return rate of **0.25%**, consistent across all regions — indicating excellent product quality
- **Bob** emerged as the top-performing salesperson
- **Tablet** and **Laptop** are the leading products in both sales and profit

## Dashboard Features

### Sales Trend Analysis
- Year-wise sales comparison (2023–2025)
- Interactive area chart showing sales trend over time

### Regional Analysis
- Return percentage analysis by region (North, Central, East, West, South)

### Sales Team Performance
- Ranking of top sales persons with horizontal bar chart

### Product Performance
- Side-by-side comparison of Total Sales vs Total Profit for all products

### Payment Behavior
- Distribution of sales by Mode of Payment using an intuitive pie chart
